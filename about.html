WHO WE ARE

More Than a Bar & Restaurant — A Destination

Wilson Palace was founded with a clear ambition: to create an upscale yet welcoming social palace where friends, families, and sports lovers gather for unforgettable culinary and nightlife experiences.

Whether you stop by at lunchtime for a hearty plate of Sombe ya Kinono with Bougali, gather with friends over sizzling charcoal brochettes and Poulet Mayo, or cheer for your favorite club in our HD Football Arena, every detail at Wilson Palace is crafted for excellence.

Our pride extends beyond the kitchen into our signature beverage line—led by Wilson 100% Naturel Maracuja—and our exclusive line of branded lifestyle accessories.
